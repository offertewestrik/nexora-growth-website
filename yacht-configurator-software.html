Yacht configurator software for shipyards, brokers and boat dealers.

The brands that lead this market, Prestige, Hanse, Arcona, Dynamiq, all let buyers build their own yacht online: pick a model, hull colour, deck, interior and options, and walk into the showroom with a configuration already in hand. Most yards, brokers and dealers still have nothing comparable. That is the gap this platform closes: boat configurator software that runs on your domain, in your brand, with your model programme.

A Next Growth yacht configurator goes beyond colour swatches. It draws five live views from the vessel's real dimensions, computes a cruising profile, speed, range, licence rules, berth size, and turns every configuration into a branded PDF dossier with a configuration code and QR code that reopens the exact build. That code is the lead: instead of an empty contact form, every enquiry arrives with the buyer's complete specification, model, colours, propulsion and options, ready for your CRM. It is lead generation built into the product, not bolted on.

The same platform scales down to tenders and motorboats and up to a 3D yacht configurator when your imagery is ready for it. You can go live before a single render exists, the system draws every view from dimensions, generates a complete render list from your model programme, and each photorealistic render that arrives automatically replaces the drawing for exactly that combination. Never a different boat, never a wrong colour.

Veelgestelde vragen

Wat je vooraf wilt weten.

What does a yacht configurator cost?

It depends on the imagery you want: the drawn views in the demo work from day one, photorealistic renders are added in phases, and a fully rotatable 3D model is the top tier. In a strategy call we map your model programme and give you a concrete quote. The demo shows exactly what you get, try it first.

Do we need renders before we can launch?

No, and that is deliberate. The configurator draws every view live from your models' dimensions, so it is fully functional before a single render exists. Renders are added in phases afterwards; every render that arrives automatically replaces the drawing for exactly that combination. You can be live while the imagery is still in production.

Are the cruising-profile calculations trustworthy?

The rules are public and verifiable: displacement speed follows the classic hull-speed formula, range follows from tank capacity and consumption with a 10% reserve, and licence thresholds come directly from the Dutch CBR rules, for other flags we substitute the applicable regime. Your own figures come from sea trials and replace the sample values; until then every sheet carries a SAMPLE MODEL stamp.

Should we show prices in this segment at all?

You decide with a single switch. Off, the configurator shows “price on request” everywhere and the enquiry button keeps working, often the most credible choice in the premium segment. On, with your price table filled in, it prices every option and keeps a running total.

How does the enquiry reach us?

The configuration travels with the enquiry: every build has a code embedded in the link and printed in the dossier, which reopens it exactly. Buyers share their build with a partner or broker through the same link. We connect the configurator to your CRM, as we do for our other configurators.

Can it run in multiple languages?

Yes. All copy, choices, sheets, cruising profile and dossier, lives in a single layer delivered per language. The demo runs in Dutch; your configurator ships in the languages you sell in.

Whose domain does it run on, and who owns the data?

Yours. It runs on your domain in your brand, and your models, specifications and prices live in one data file you manage yourself.

Does it work as a boat configurator for tenders and motorboats?

Yes. The platform is not tied to a length class: the same structure, choices, live imagery, calculation modules, dossier, works for tenders and motorboats just as well as for a sixty-metre yacht. The cruising profile computes from the smallest size up.

Does a configurator actually generate leads?

That is what it is built for. Every configuration carries a code embedded in the share link, the QR code and the PDF dossier, so each enquiry arrives with the buyer's complete specification, model, colours, propulsion, options. Your sales conversation starts with their yacht on the table, not with an empty intake form. It is the same reason the leading brands run configurators as the first step of their sales funnel.
