For pool builders

From "how much is a pool?"
to a complete request.

PoolStudio is an online pool designer that Next Growth builds for pool builders. It runs on your own website, in your branding, with your catalog and your prices. Your customer designs the pool at the kitchen table. You get a lead that is already specified.

50 seconds, recorded live in the three demos, nothing stagedPrices update on screen as options are clicked

What your customer does

No forms, no waiting for a callback. Three steps, on any phone.

1

Uploads a photo of the backyard

The pool is placed into their own yard, so they see it in the space they actually have. Not in a stock photo of somebody else's garden.

2

Picks shape, size and finish

Shape, dimensions, liner color, entry steps, decking, cover, heating and pool house. A realistic image updates at every choice.

3

Sees a live price in dollars

Sized in feet and inches, priced from your own numbers. They know the ballpark before they ever pick up the phone.

What you get

Every request lands in your inbox fully specified: model, dimensions, finish, entry steps, options and the calculated price. The "what does a pool cost?" call disappears, and the people who do reach out are already qualified.

Your brand, not ours

Your logo, colors and typography. Customers never see a third-party tool. It looks and feels like part of your site.

Your catalog, your prices

We load your models, options and pricing. You keep control: change a price, and the configurator changes with it.

Live in two weeks

We build it, you review it, it goes on your site. No integration project, no development team needed on your side.

Click through it yourself

Three live demos, all in English, sized in feet and priced in dollars. Change a finish, a size or an option, and the image and the price move with you. Your version carries your branding, your models and your numbers.
